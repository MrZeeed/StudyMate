About Us

StudyMate UK is a student support community for international students in the UK.

Why we exist

Studying in a new country is exciting, and it can also feel overwhelming. There is a lot to learn, and it is not always easy to know where to start or who to ask.

StudyMate UK brings international students together so that nobody has to work it all out alone. We share clear information, practical tips and friendly encouragement.

Who it is for

Our community is for international students who are planning to study in the UK, have just arrived, or are already part of university life.

Please note: StudyMate UK does not give visa, immigration or legal advice. For those questions, speak to your university’s international office or use official sources.

What we care about

Student Support

Five areas where StudyMate UK can help you on your UK student journey.

University applications

Applying can feel complicated. We help you understand the steps and get organised.

  • Understand how UK applications work
  • Prepare your documents and personal statement
  • Know what to ask universities before you apply

Course selection

The right course is about more than the title. Think about what you enjoy and where you want to go next.

  • Compare subjects, course types and teaching styles
  • Check entry requirements carefully
  • Talk to students who have taken similar courses

Accommodation

A comfortable home makes studying easier. Take your time and check the details.

  • Learn about university housing and private renting
  • Read contracts carefully before you sign
  • Plan your budget for rent and bills

Student life

Settling in takes time, and it is normal to feel unsure at first.

  • Meet people through societies and community events
  • Find your way around your new city
  • Look after your wellbeing and ask for help early

Career preparation

Start thinking about your future early so you can make the most of your time at university.

  • Build a strong CV and develop your skills
  • Explore internships and work experience
  • Use your university careers service

FAQs

Answers to common questions about StudyMate UK.

What is StudyMate UK?

StudyMate UK is a student support community that helps international students with their journey to and through UK higher education.

Who can join?

International students who are planning to study in the UK, have just arrived, or are already studying here.

What topics can you help with?

University applications, course selection, accommodation, student life and career preparation.

Do you give visa or legal advice?

No. For visa, immigration or legal questions, please contact your university’s international office or use official sources such as GOV.UK and UKCISA.
